Corner lots get a reputation for being busy. This one earns a different reputation entirely. Set at the quiet intersection of Elmwood Avenue and a dead-end side street, 9 Elmwood benefits from the space and light of a corner position without the traffic. The result is a property that feels larger than its footprint and more private than its neighborhood density would suggest.

## What makes this property exceptional

This is a well-proportioned colonial with honest bones and a series of smart, practical updates that make it genuinely ready to live in. No dramatic renovations, no staging tricks, just a well-maintained home in a neighborhood where properties rarely become available and, when they do, don't stay available for long.

## Interior highlights

- 3 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms across two levels
- Updated kitchen with granite countertops, tile backsplash, and newer appliances
- Hardwood floors throughout the main level, refinished in 2022
- Living room with a wood-burning fireplace and large picture window
- Sun-filled dining room with direct access to the rear deck
- Primary bedroom with a walk-in closet and private en suite bathroom
- Full basement, partially finished with a home office nook

## Outdoor and grounds

The corner lot provides a wraparound yard with frontage on both streets, substantially more outdoor space than neighboring properties of a similar footprint. A rear deck off the dining room is the natural gathering point for warm months. The two-car driveway accommodates off-street parking comfortably.

## Location

Riverside is one of Greenwich's quieter, more residential villages, close enough to the water to feel coastal, and close enough to the Post Road to be genuinely convenient. The Riverside train station is a 9-minute walk.
