On the twelfth floor of one of downtown Greenwich's best-regarded residential buildings, Unit 12C offers something that is genuinely difficult to find at this price point in this market: space, views, and a walk-to-everything location in a single package. The unit faces south and west, capturing afternoon light across the living and dining areas and a skyline view that extends toward Long Island Sound on clear days.

## What makes this property exceptional

Greenwich Terrace is a full-service building with a level of management and common area quality that is consistently maintained rather than periodically refreshed. The building has undergone significant capital improvements over the past three years, including a new roof, updated lobby, and a fully renovated fitness center. Unit 12C itself has been lightly updated by the current owner with fresh paint, new light fixtures throughout, and a renovated primary bathroom completed in 2023.

## Building and common areas

- 2 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms on a single open-plan level
- Generous living and dining area with floor-to-ceiling windows on two sides
- Kitchen with granite countertops, stainless steel appliances, and a breakfast bar
- Primary suite with a renovated bathroom featuring a soaking tub and separate shower
- Guest bedroom with a full bathroom and its own linen closet
- Two assigned underground parking spaces included
- Private storage unit on the basement level

Greenwich Terrace offers 24-hour concierge service, a landscaped rooftop terrace with seating and a gas grill station, a fitness center, a residents-only library and meeting room, and a climate-controlled wine storage room available to residents. The building is pet-friendly with approval.

## Location

88 Mason Street places residents within a 4-minute walk of Greenwich Avenue's full range of restaurants, specialty retailers, and coffee shops. Greenwich train station is a 7-minute walk, with express service to New York Penn Station in under 50 minutes. This unit suits buyers seeking a low-maintenance primary residence or a well-located pied-a-terre.
