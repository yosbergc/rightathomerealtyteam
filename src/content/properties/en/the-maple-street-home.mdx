Some homes ask you to imagine what they could be. 22 Maple Street doesn't need to ask. Freshly updated, move-in ready, and sitting on a level half-acre lot in one of Cos Cob's most established residential streets, this is the kind of home that buyers move into on a Friday and feel settled by Sunday.

## What makes this property exceptional

The owners have lived here for eleven years and maintained it with a level of care that shows in every room. The 2023 kitchen renovation, the updated primary bathroom, the newly refinished hardwood floors throughout, none of it was done to sell. It was done to live in. That distinction is visible the moment you walk through the door.

## Interior highlights

- 4 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms across two levels
- Renovated kitchen with quartz countertops, shaker cabinetry, and stainless appliances
- Open-plan kitchen and family room with a gas fireplace, the home's social center
- Formal dining room with original built-in china cabinet
- Primary suite with an updated en suite bathroom and two closets
- Full unfinished basement with high ceilings, significant expansion potential
- Attached two-car garage with interior access

## Outdoor and grounds

The rear yard is flat, fully fenced, and large enough for a pool addition without sacrificing lawn space. A bluestone patio off the family room provides an easy transition between indoor and outdoor living. The mature shade trees along the rear property line provide natural privacy without requiring any maintenance.

## Location

Maple Street is a 7-minute walk to Cos Cob train station, providing direct Metro-North service to Grand Central Terminal. The Eastern Greenwich Civic Center, Cos Cob Park, and the area's best local dining are all within a 10-minute radius.
