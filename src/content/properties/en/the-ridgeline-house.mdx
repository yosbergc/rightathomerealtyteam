Set on a privately gated 1.2-acre lot at the end of a quiet cul-de-sac, The Ridgeline House is the kind of property that earns its reputation before you step through the door. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame unobstructed views of the Long Island Sound from both the main living level and the primary suite, a perspective that changes with every season and never loses its effect.

## What makes this property exceptional

The home was designed with a clear intention: to make the interior feel continuous with the landscape. The open-plan living and dining area opens directly onto a 1,400 sqft wraparound terrace through a full wall of sliding glass panels. The result is a home that feels significantly larger than its footprint suggests, and one that works equally well for quiet mornings and formal entertaining.

## Interior highlights

- 5 bedrooms, 5.5 bathrooms across three levels
- Chef's kitchen with Calacatta marble island, dual ovens, and a butler's pantry
- Primary suite with a private terrace, spa bath with heated floors, and a walk-in dressing room
- Home office with built-in shelving and a dedicated entrance, suitable for client meetings
- Lower level recreation room with a wet bar and direct access to the pool terrace

## Outdoor and grounds

The grounds have been professionally landscaped with a mature privacy hedge along the perimeter, a heated in-ground pool and spa, and a pool house with a full bathroom. A four-car attached garage with EV charging completes the property.

## Location

Ridgeline Court sits within a 12-minute drive of Greenwich Avenue, offering access to the area's best restaurants, boutiques, and commuter rail connections to New York Penn Station in under 50 minutes.
