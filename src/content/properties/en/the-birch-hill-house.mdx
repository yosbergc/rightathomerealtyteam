Not every great home is large. The Birch Hill House makes a compelling case for the well-considered smaller home, a property where every room has a purpose, every square foot is accounted for, and nothing feels wasted. For buyers who are right-sizing, buying for the first time, or simply prioritizing location over square footage, this is worth a close look.

## What makes this property exceptional

The home has been owned by the same family for nearly two decades and shows the kind of care that comes with long-term ownership rather than investor preparation. The 2021 roof replacement, the updated electrical panel, the newer water heater, these are the improvements that matter most and are often invisible in a listing until something goes wrong. Here, they've already been done.

## Interior highlights

- 3 bedrooms, 1.5 bathrooms across two levels
- Renovated kitchen with butcher block countertops, open shelving, and a farmhouse sink
- Bright living room with original hardwood floors and a large south-facing window
- Dedicated dining area with built-in window bench seating
- Primary bedroom with a double closet and ample natural light
- Updated half bathroom on the main level, rare at this price point
- Partial basement for storage and laundry

## Outdoor and grounds

The rear yard is modestly sized but well laid out, a paved patio, a small lawn, and a raised garden bed along the rear fence. Low maintenance without feeling sparse. The detached one-car garage doubles as a workshop or additional storage space.

## Location

Birch Hill Road sits at the Greenwich-Stamford border, offering the best of both addresses, Greenwich school district access with Stamford's broader amenity base. The downtown Stamford Metro-North hub is 12 minutes by car, with express trains to Grand Central running throughout the day.
