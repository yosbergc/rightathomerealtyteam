Stone Gate Villa announces itself quietly, a fieldstone entry gate, a long gravel drive, and then the house itself, a substantial shingle-style home set well back from the road on 1.8 acres of landscaped grounds. It is the kind of property that rewards patience: the more time you spend here, the more considered every detail reveals itself to be.

## What makes this property exceptional

Originally built in 1998 and comprehensively renovated in 2022, Stone Gate Villa blends the architectural character of a classic Greenwich estate with the functionality of a thoroughly modern home. The renovation touched every major system, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, while preserving the original stone fireplaces, wide-plank oak floors, and the generous room proportions that define the home's character.

## Interior highlights

- 6 bedrooms, 6 bathrooms across three levels
- Grand entrance foyer with a curved staircase and original millwork
- Formal living room with a wood-burning fireplace and coffered ceiling
- Kitchen renovated with custom cabinetry, Thermador appliances, and a 12-foot island
- Finished third floor with a media room, gym space, and additional storage
- Three-zone climate control, fully updated in 2022

## Outdoor and grounds

The rear grounds feature a bluestone terrace, a covered outdoor kitchen with built-in grill and refrigeration, and a 50-foot heated pool. A detached two-bedroom guest cottage with its own full kitchen and entrance sits at the rear of the property, suited for extended family, long-term guests, or a home office that genuinely separates work from home.

## Location

Stonebridge Lane is located within the North Street School district, one of the most sought-after elementary assignments in the Greenwich public school system. The Merritt Parkway is 8 minutes away, and the Greenwich train station is a 14-minute drive.
