There are properties that were designed to impress, and there are properties that were designed to be lived in beautifully. The Glass Pavilion, a single-level contemporary home on a quiet Harbor View Drive lot, belongs firmly in the second category. Architect-designed in 2019 and owner-occupied since completion, it has never been on the market until now.

## What makes this property exceptional

The home's defining feature is its relationship to light. Positioned on an east-west axis, the floor plan ensures natural light moves through the interior from sunrise to sunset without ever creating glare in the primary living spaces. South-facing clerestory windows along the roofline supplement the floor-to-ceiling glazing in a way that keeps the home bright year-round without sacrificing privacy.

## Interior highlights

- 4 bedrooms, 3.5 bathrooms on a single level, fully accessible throughout
- Open-plan kitchen, dining, and living area spanning 1,100 sqft
- Kitchen by Bulthaup with integrated appliances and a poured concrete island
- Primary suite positioned at the western end of the home for maximum privacy
- Two additional bedroom wings at the eastern end, ideal for children or guests
- Radiant heated concrete floors throughout the main living area

## Outdoor and grounds

A 900 sqft covered outdoor living room, fully screened and equipped with a fireplace, ceiling fans, and built-in audio, extends the home's usable footprint for three seasons of the year. The landscaped rear garden features a lap pool, perimeter lighting, and a vegetable garden with raised cedar beds.

## Location

Old Greenwich offers a distinct character within the broader Greenwich area, walkable to Perrot Memorial Library, Greenwich Point Park, and the Old Greenwich train station. New York City is 45 minutes by express train.
