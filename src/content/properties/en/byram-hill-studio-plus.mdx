Unit 2A at 5 Byram Shore Road represents one of the most accessible entry points into Greenwich homeownership currently on the market. At 1,050 square feet, it is meaningfully larger than most studio and junior one-bedroom units in the area, with a layout that functions as a genuine one-bedroom thanks to a partial dividing wall that creates a defined sleeping area without sacrificing the open feel of the living space.

## What makes this property exceptional

This unit was updated in 2020 with practical improvements that make a real difference in daily living. The kitchen was fully replaced with new cabinetry, appliances, and a tile backsplash. The bathroom was retiled and fitted with a new vanity and fixtures. The original hardwood floors were refinished throughout. The result is a unit that is genuinely move-in ready and requires no immediate investment beyond what the buyer chooses to personalize.

## Building and common areas

- Open-plan studio with a defined sleeping alcove, functioning as a one-bedroom layout
- Fully renovated kitchen with white cabinetry, quartz countertops, and a gas range
- Renovated bathroom with subway tile, a new vanity, and updated fixtures
- Refinished hardwood floors throughout
- Large double-pane windows with southern exposure and good natural light
- In-unit air conditioning units in the sleeping and living areas
- One assigned outdoor parking space included

5 Byram Shore Road is a 16-unit boutique building with a low monthly HOA fee that covers water, trash, and common area maintenance. The building has a shared laundry facility on the ground floor and a landscaped courtyard with seating. The building is owner-managed with a responsive maintenance team.

## Location

Byram Shore Road runs along the western edge of Greenwich near the Byram neighborhood, offering a quieter residential setting with proximity to Byram Park and the shoreline. The Port Chester train station is a 10-minute walk, providing Metro-North access to Grand Central. This unit is well suited to first-time buyers, young professionals, or investors seeking a low-maintenance income property in a supply-constrained market.
