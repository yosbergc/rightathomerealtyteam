The suite has been owner-occupied by a small financial advisory firm for the past eight years and reflects the kind of ongoing investment that comes with genuine ownership pride. Valley Road runs through the heart of Cos Cob's commercial district, within a 5-minute drive of I-95 and the Merritt Parkway.
