Unit 4B at 210 River Road is a fourth-floor corner condominium with direct views of Cos Cob Harbor from both the living room and the primary bedroom. It is the kind of unit that becomes available once every few years in this building and rarely stays available for long. The combination of water views, updated interiors, and a commuter-friendly location makes it one of the most practical and genuinely pleasant places to live in the Greenwich area at this price point.

## What makes this property exceptional

The unit was fully renovated by the current owner in 2021 with a clear brief: make it feel like a home, not a rental. The result is a space that is warm, considered, and immediately livable. New wide-plank flooring runs throughout the entire unit. The kitchen was reconfigured to open onto the living area, which improved both the layout and the sense of space significantly.

## Building and common areas

- 2 bedrooms, 2 full bathrooms on a single level
- Renovated open-plan kitchen with quartz countertops, white shaker cabinets, and a peninsula with seating for three
- Primary bedroom with harbor views, a walk-in closet, and an en suite bathroom with a frameless glass shower
- Second bedroom currently configured as a home office with custom built-in shelving
- In-unit laundry with a full-size washer and dryer
- Central air conditioning replaced in 2022
- One assigned covered parking space included

210 River Road is a well-maintained 24-unit building with a responsive on-site property manager. The building features a rooftop terrace with seating and water views, a secure package room, a fitness room on the second floor, and a guest parking area. Monthly HOA fees cover water, trash, landscaping, snow removal, and building insurance.

## Location

The building sits a 6-minute walk from Cos Cob train station, with direct Metro-North service to Grand Central Terminal. Greenwich Avenue is 8 minutes by car. The unit is particularly well suited to commuters, remote workers, or buyers looking to enter the Greenwich market without the maintenance responsibilities of a single-family home.
